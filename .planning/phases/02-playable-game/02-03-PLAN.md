| T-02-03 | Tampering | click after game over | mitigate | Overlay (`position: absolute; inset: 0`) covers the board, all cells get `.cell--frozen` (pointer-events none) and `interactive=false`, engine returns `GameAlreadyOver`; verified at checkpoint step 5 |
| T-02-04 | Denial of Service | `GameOverOverlay` | mitigate | `outcome_text` returns `Option` and the component renders nothing for InProgress instead of `unreachable!` (grep gate in Task 1) |
