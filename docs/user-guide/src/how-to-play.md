# How to play

## The rules

Tic-tac-toe is played on a 3x3 grid. Take turns placing your mark in an empty cell. The first to get three in a row, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, wins. If the board fills up with no line, the game is a draw.

## Your game

You are X and you always move first: click any empty cell to place your mark. The engine plays O and answers instantly. Occupied cells cannot be clicked, and the board locks when the game ends.

The engine plays perfectly, so the best you can do is a draw, and it punishes mistakes. A training mode that lets you win and gives hints is planned for a later release.

## Tips

- Take the centre or a corner on your first move.
- Block the engine whenever it has two in a row.
- Watch for forks, where one move creates two threats at once.
